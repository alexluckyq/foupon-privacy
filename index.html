Foupon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Foupon ("Foupon", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Foupon Android app and its related services. The app is operated by Restaurant Coupons Canada. Foupon helps users find restaurant offers, coupons, nearby businesses, and gas prices. Some features are available without signing in.

1. Information we handle

  • Location and address information: With your device permission, the app accesses your approximate or precise location. We also process addresses you type or select, coordinates, province, and postal information to find nearby offers and gas stations, show maps, and improve address suggestions. The app saves your selected location on your device. Location information is also included in some analytics events.
  • Account information: If you register or sign in, we process information such as your name, email address, account identifier, authentication credentials or tokens, and profile information made available by your chosen Google or Facebook sign-in provider. Merchant features also process merchant account and business information.
  • App activity and diagnostics: We and our service providers process app interactions, viewed content, feature use, device and operating-system information, app version, crash reports, performance information, and network information such as IP addresses. Analytics may include your Foupon account identifier when signed in and location-related information.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad activity: Advertising services may process your Android advertising ID, other app or device identifiers, IP address, ad impressions, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads, analyze performance, and detect fraud.
  • Notifications: Firebase services process app-instance or installation identifiers and registration tokens to support push notifications. Android notification permission controls whether notifications can be displayed.
  • Camera and local files: Merchant QR-code scanning uses the camera with permission and processes scanned codes for the relevant merchant or redemption feature. The app also uses local storage for settings, cached content, downloaded content, and saved location information; some older Android versions may request storage permission.
  • Support messages: When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and any attachments.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide location-based offers and gas prices; complete address searches; display maps and content; authenticate users and operate account, rewards, and merchant features; deliver notifications; respond to support requests; diagnose problems and improve the app; display and measure advertising; and protect our services against abuse and fraud.

3. Service providers and sharing

The app communicates with Foupon backend services hosted using Amazon Web Services and with third-party services needed for the features you use. Those providers receive the information necessary to process requests, including network information such as your IP address.

  • Google: Google Mobile Ads provides advertising; Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics provide analytics and crash reporting; Firebase Cloud Messaging supports notifications; Google Maps and location services provide maps and location functionality; and Google Sign-In supports optional sign-in. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.
  • Meta/Facebook: The app includes the Facebook SDK for optional sign-in and related SDK functionality. Meta may receive identifiers, device information, and app events under its SDK settings and policies. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Geoapify: Address autocomplete sends your search text and, when available, coordinates used to prioritize nearby suggestions to Geoapify. See Geoapify's Privacy Policy.
  • GasBuddy: Gas-price searches communicate with GasBuddy services using the requested search area. GasBuddy receives the request and associated network information. See GasBuddy's Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Web Services: AWS hosts Foupon's backend infrastructure. See AWS's Privacy Notice.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect users or our services, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations. When you open an external website, map app, or merchant service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

4. Your choices and permissions

You can manage location, camera, storage, and notification permissions in Android Settings. You can enter a different address instead of using your current location. Disabling a permission may prevent the associated feature from working. You can manage advertising-ID controls in your device's privacy or advertising settings, where available. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising, analytics, or other data processing.

You can sign out of Foupon and manage third-party sign-in access through your Google or Facebook account. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local app data, but does not by itself delete your Foupon account or information held by our servers or service providers.

5. Retention, access, and deletion requests

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing accounts and services, resolving support requests, protecting against abuse, and meeting legal obligations. Retention varies by data type, account status, and the service provider involved. Cached information on your device may be replaced or removed as the app operates. Service providers may apply their own retention rules.

To ask about your information or request access, correction, deletion of your Foupon account, or deletion of associated data, email support@foupon.co. For account deletion, use the subject "Foupon account deletion" and identify the account email address or sign-in provider. Do not send your password. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be excluded from deletion; we will explain applicable limitations when responding.

6. Security and international processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your own because Foupon and its service providers operate online infrastructure in multiple locations. We use safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

Foupon is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. We will publish the updated policy here and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Foupon at support@foupon.co.
