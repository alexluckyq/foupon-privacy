Foupon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how Foupon ("Foupon", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Foupon Android and iOS apps and their related services. The apps are operated by Foupon Inc. Foupon helps users find restaurant offers, coupons, nearby businesses, and gas prices. Information handling and available controls vary by platform, app version, and the features you use.

Starting with Android version code 2801 (version 2.800), the Android app does not offer account creation, sign-in, rewards accounts, or a merchant portal. The iOS 2.800 app also does not offer account creation or sign-in. The sections about earlier account and merchant features describe versions where those features were available and information previously provided to us. Android session cleanup described below is specific to Android; updating the iOS app does not automatically perform that cleanup.

1. Information we handle

  • Location and address information: With your device permission, the app accesses your approximate or precise location. We also process addresses you type or select, coordinates, province, and postal information to find nearby offers and gas stations, show maps, and improve address suggestions. The app saves your selected location on your device. Nearby-offer requests send coordinates to Foupon's backend, and gas-price requests send the requested coordinates to GasBuddy. Android includes coordinates and postal information in some analytics events and uses province and postal-prefix analytics properties. In iOS version 2.800, app-generated location and deal analytics events omit coordinates and full postal codes, and the app removes its postal-prefix analytics property. Province information remains in iOS analytics and may be used to select notifications relevant to your region. Earlier iOS versions included more detailed location information in analytics.
  • Account information from earlier versions: Earlier versions allowed registration and sign-in and processed information such as your name, email address, account identifier, authentication credentials or tokens, and profile information made available by your chosen Google or Facebook sign-in provider. Earlier merchant features also processed merchant account and business information. Android builds starting with version code 2801 remove local saved consumer and merchant sessions and no longer send a Foupon account identifier to analytics. This Android cleanup does not delete information already held by our backend or providers. The iOS app does not automatically perform the same session cleanup; information associated with earlier versions may remain on the device or with Foupon and its providers.
  • App activity and diagnostics: We and our service providers process app interactions, viewed content, feature use, device and operating-system information, app version, crash reports, performance information, and network information such as IP addresses. The location information included in analytics varies by platform and version as described above. Earlier signed-in versions may also have included your Foupon account identifier.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad activity: Advertising services may process your Android advertising ID or, on iOS when you grant Apple's tracking permission, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). They may also process other app or device identifiers, IP address, ad impressions, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide and measure ads, analyze performance, and detect fraud. On iOS version 2.800, the app requests non-personalized ads when tracking permission is denied or restricted and ads are otherwise permitted by the advertising consent flow. Declining tracking does not stop all advertising or other information processing.
  • Notifications: Firebase services process app-instance or installation identifiers and registration tokens to support push notifications. Your device notification permission controls whether notifications can be displayed. On iOS, Apple's push notification service also supports delivery. We may use your province to select relevant notifications; this does not require permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Local files and earlier camera features: Earlier Android merchant QR-code scanning used the camera with permission and processed scanned codes for merchant or redemption features. This Android camera feature was removed in version code 2801. The app uses local storage for settings, cached content, downloaded content, and saved location information; some older Android versions may request storage permission.
  • Support messages: When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and any attachments.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide location-based offers and gas prices; complete address searches; display maps and content; operate account, rewards, and merchant features in earlier versions and handle related support or deletion requests; deliver notifications, including province-based messages; respond to support requests; diagnose problems and improve the app; display and measure advertising; and protect our services against abuse and fraud.

3. Service providers and sharing

The app communicates with Foupon backend services hosted using Amazon Web Services and with third-party services needed for the features you use. Those providers receive the information necessary to process requests, including network information such as your IP address.

  • Google: On both platforms, Google Mobile Ads provides advertising, Firebase Analytics provides analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging supports notifications, and Google Maps provides maps. Firebase Crashlytics provides crash reporting in the Android app; the iOS 2.800 app does not include Crashlytics. Android also uses Google location services. The iOS app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices. Earlier versions also used Google Sign-In for optional sign-in. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.
  • Apple on iOS: Apple's location, geocoding, and map-search services support location lookup and address suggestions, and Apple's push notification service supports notification delivery. These services may process location or search information and information needed to deliver notifications. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta/Facebook in earlier versions: Earlier versions included the Facebook SDK for sign-in and related SDK functionality, through which Meta may have received identifiers, device information, and app events. The Facebook sign-in SDK was removed from Android version code 2801; iOS 2.800 also does not include the Facebook sign-in SDK. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Geoapify on Android: Android address autocomplete sends your search text and, when available, coordinates used to prioritize nearby suggestions to Geoapify. See Geoapify's Privacy Policy.
  • GasBuddy: Gas-price searches communicate with GasBuddy services using the requested search area. GasBuddy receives the request and associated network information. See GasBuddy's Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Web Services: AWS hosts Foupon's backend infrastructure. See AWS's Privacy Notice.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect users or our services, or in connection with a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations. When you open an external website, map app, or merchant service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

4. Your choices and permissions

You can enter a different address instead of using your current location. Disabling a permission may prevent the associated feature from working. Permission controls and advertising choices do not necessarily stop all advertising, analytics, or other information processing.

Android

You can manage location, storage, and notification permissions in Android Settings; camera permission applies only to earlier Android merchant versions. You can manage advertising-ID controls in your device's privacy or advertising settings, where available.

iOS

You can manage Foupon's location, notification, and tracking permissions in iOS Settings. In version 2.800, Foupon requests Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before enabling tracking for advertising. You can continue using Foupon if you decline. The app requests ads only when Google's advertising consent flow permits them; declining or restricting tracking results in non-personalized ad requests when ads are otherwise permitted.

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform provides advertising privacy choices. When a privacy-options entry is required, Privacy Choices appears in Foupon's Settings so you can review and change those choices. iOS tracking permission and regional advertising consent are separate controls. You can change an existing tracking decision in iOS Settings.

The iOS 2.800 app configures Firebase Analytics advertising-storage, advertising-user-data, and advertising-personalization consent as disabled. App-usage analytics and province-based notification targeting remain enabled independently of the ATT decision. Turning off tracking does not turn off these analytics or prevent necessary location requests for nearby features.

Earlier accounts and local data

If you used an earlier account-enabled version, you can manage third-party sign-in access through your Google or Facebook account. Updating to Android version code 2801 or later clears saved Android consumer and merchant sessions on the device; the iOS app does not perform that same cleanup. Removing an app or clearing local app data, where supported, does not by itself delete information held by Foupon's servers or service providers. You can request deletion as described below.

5. Retention, access, and deletion requests

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including providing accounts and services, resolving support requests, protecting against abuse, and meeting legal obligations. Retention varies by data type, account status, and the service provider involved. Cached information on your device may be replaced or removed as the app operates. Service providers may apply their own retention rules.

To ask about your information or request access, correction, deletion of your Foupon account, or deletion of associated data, email support@foupon.com. For account deletion, use the subject "Foupon account deletion" and identify the account email address or sign-in provider. Do not send your password. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Information that must be retained for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons may be excluded from deletion; we will explain applicable limitations when responding.

6. Security and international processing

Information may be processed in countries other than your own because Foupon and its service providers operate online infrastructure in multiple locations. We use safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

Foupon is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. We will publish the updated policy here and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Foupon at support@foupon.com.
